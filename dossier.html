1. Executive Summary & Transformation Journey

This dossier documents the comprehensive modernization and commercial deployment of Melete, transitioning it from a static frontend demonstration into a high-scale, production-grade Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform.

The initiative began by benchmarking Melete against ExamTestAI (a commercial Pre-CAS platform for Nepali students applying to the UK). While ExamTestAI had market-validated feedback loops ("What we heard you say" and natural English rewrites), Melete possessed a superior editorial design system and broader multi-country ambition. We bridged the technical gap by removing all mock/simulated behaviors, implementing a robust Node.js backend with native REST APIs, integrating real-time audio capture, connecting the OpenAI GPT-4o-mini and Whisper APIs, engineering an administrative control center, and deploying the platform live to Vercel.

Core Milestone Achieved
Melete is fully deployed and publicly accessible with real-time speech evaluation against international credibility rubrics, active Google login tracking, dynamic university question banks, and zero student fees.

3. Technical & System Architecture

Melete was engineered with a decoupled, high-performance architecture capable of running locally or at the edge via serverless environments:

[Client Layer] ──> Audio Recording (MediaRecorder) + Web Speech API + 60fps Canvas │ ▼ (HTTPS POST multipart/form-data) [Node.js / Express Server] (Vercel Serverless Function Adapter) │ ├─► /api/transcribe ──► OpenAI Whisper-1 Audio API ──► Verbatim Transcript ├─► /api/evaluate ──► OpenAI GPT-4o-mini API ──► Comparative Feedback JSON ├─► /api/auth ──► Google Identity Service ──► Persistent Candidate Tracking └─► /api/admin/* ──► Data Engine (melete_db.json)──► Analytics & Question Bank CRUD

Key Architectural Components:

  • Real-Time Audio & Video Ingest: Microphone audio is captured via navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia, processed through an AudioContext frequency analyzer that powers a 48-bar visualizer, and recorded in WebM/WAV buffers for server upload. Video mode activates client-side WebRTC camera streams.
  • Silence & Audibility Guardian: Incorporates an audio sanity check. If ambient noise or silence is recorded (< 4 words), the engine halts evaluation and prompts: "We couldn't hear your response clearly. Please check your mic and try again."
  • Dual-Engine AI Pipeline:
    • OpenAI Whisper-1: Transcribes uploaded audio blobs with international accent resilience.
    • OpenAI GPT-4o-mini: Analyzes candidate statements against regulatory requirements (working hour limits, course modules, liquid maintenance, non-immigrant intent).
    • Intelligent Heuristic Fallback: Runs a rule-based semantic parser if network or API keys are unavailable, guaranteeing 100% platform uptime.
  • Serverless-Ready Data Layer: Utilizes an atomic JSON database module (db.js) configured with automatic detection for Vercel's ephemeral /tmp storage, ensuring zero file-system collision or write-permission errors.

4. Core Application Features

4.1. Single-Question Drill Mode (practice.html)

Designed for fast, high-repetition practice. Students select their target country and university, hit record, and answer a single focused prompt (e.g. "Who is your financial sponsor and how do they earn their income?"). Within 3 seconds of completion, the system generates:

  • Verbatim Transcript ("What We Heard You Say"): Exposes grammatical slips and mispronounced numbers.
  • Natural Spoken Alternative ("A Better Way to Say It"): Conversational, confident English phrasing.
  • Audio Replay Player: Allows students to hear their own hesitations and pacing.
  • Red-Flag Warning Audit: Highlights critical omissions (e.g. failing to cite the 28-day bank balance rule).

4.2. Full 15-Question Mock Interview (session.html)

Simulates genuine consular and university interview conditions. Features webcam streaming, consecutive question progression, speech synthesis prompt delivery, and a comprehensive end-of-session diagnostic report (report.html).

4.3. University-Specific Question Bank

Pre-loaded with institutional questions mapped from published university guidelines and student interview debriefs:

  • United Kingdom: Coventry University, BPP, University of East London, University of West London, University of Wolverhampton, University of Hertfordshire, Ravensbourne, and UKVI Home Office.
  • United States: New York University (NYU), Northeastern University, Texas A&M, US Consular 214(b) drills.
  • Canada: University of Toronto, Conestoga College (SDS & GIC requirements).
  • Germany: Technical University of Munich (TUM), RWTH Aachen (Sperrkonto blocked account).
  • Australia: University of Melbourne, Deakin University (Genuine Student economic metrics).
