Our Purpose • Realistic Practice

Confident Answers Come From Real Knowledge, Not Memorized Scripts.

In Greek mythology, Melete was the muse of thought, practice, and careful thought. We built this platform because students struggle in interviews not from a lack of ability, but simply because they have never practiced saying their answers out loud under pressure.

Our Guiding Standard

The Three Principles of Melete

01

No Memorized Scripts

Visa officers and university admissions teams can tell when an answer is memorized. We never give you cookie-cutter templates. Instead, we help you explain your own background, courses, and goals in your own natural words.

02

Speaking Out Loud Matters

Reading silently feels easy, but speaking is different. When you speak out loud to an AI interviewer, you practice your voice, notice where you pause or use filler words, and build genuine speaking confidence.

03

Honest, Clear Feedback

After each interview, your report tells you directly where your answers were strong and where they need more detail or clearer facts, without confusing jargon or false praise.

gavel Independence & Transparency

Independent by Design

Melete is completely independent. We do not issue visas, we do not take agency commissions from universities, and we do not guarantee admissions. Our only mission is to give every international student a safe, realistic space to practice before their real interview.
