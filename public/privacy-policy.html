Data transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 October 2026

1. Data we process

Melete processes your Google account name, email address and profile image, optional phone and study-profile details, interview transcripts, AI feedback, scores, and session metadata. Google authenticates your account. Audio is uploaded to the Melete server in memory and sent to OpenAI for transcription; Melete does not save the recording to its public filesystem or database. When live browser captions are available, your browser's speech-recognition provider may also process audio under that provider's terms.

2. How we use and store data

We use the data to authenticate you, tailor practice questions, transcribe answers, generate educational feedback, save reports, prevent abuse, and operate the service. Account and report records are stored in the production database. The browser keeps unfinished interview configuration in session storage and clears it when the tab session ends or a report is completed.

3. Service providers and disclosure

Google provides identity services, OpenAI processes audio and answer context to produce transcripts and feedback, and the hosting and database providers process data needed to run the application. Melete does not routinely send candidate reports to immigration authorities, consulates, or universities. Data may be disclosed when legally required or needed to protect the service and its users.

4. Retention, security, and your choices

Records are retained only for the service's documented operating period and should be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed. Access is restricted by signed, secure session cookies and owner checks. You may sign out at any time and request access, correction, export, or deletion from the service operator. A verified privacy contact and exact retention schedule must be published before the service accepts real candidate data.

5. Important limits

No internet service can promise absolute security. Do not include passport numbers, bank account numbers, passwords, or unrelated sensitive documents in practice answers. This policy must be updated whenever the data flow or service providers change.
