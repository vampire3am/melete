Frequently Asked Questions & Support

Find clear answers about how Melete works, what interview types you can practice, how reports are scored, and how to prepare for your visa or university interview.

01

What is Melete?

SETUP add

Melete is an interview-practice platform for students preparing to study abroad. You choose an interview type, add details about your university and course, answer questions by voice or video, and receive a detailed PDF report afterward. Then you can retake and improve.

02

Who is Melete for?

SETUP add

Students preparing for university Pre-CAS interviews, UKVI credibility interviews, US student visa interviews, or custom destination interviews for countries like Germany, Canada, and Australia. Melete helps you practice under realistic conditions and get helpful feedback before your real interview.

03

Which interview types can I practice?

SETUP add

We support four main pathways: UK Pre-CAS University interviews, UKVI Credibility interviews, US F-1 Student Visa interviews, and a flexible Custom Destination option for countries like Germany (APS & Blocked Accounts), Canada (GIC & Study Permits), Australia (Genuine Student Requirement), and other global universities.

04

Do I have to add my real university and course details?

SETUP add

Yes. That's what makes the practice useful. If you practice with a different university, the questions won't match what you'll actually face. Use your real details so the interview is genuinely personalized to your application.

05

Can I practice with a university I'm still deciding between?

SETUP add

Yes. If you're still deciding between universities, you can practice with each one. This helps you clarify which university actually fits you.

06

How do I choose between voice and video practice?

PRACTICE add

Choose based on your real interview: Voice - If your real interview is phone or audio-only, or if you want to start with lower pressure. Video - If your real interview will be on camera, or if you want to practice the full experience. You can do both - starting with voice and moving to video.

07

Does the AI really ask follow-up questions based on what I say?

PRACTICE add

Yes. The AI listens to your answer and asks follow-ups based on what you actually said. You might get a different follow-up than another student because you gave a different answer. This is what makes it realistic.

08

How long is a typical interview?

PRACTICE add

Most interviews take 15-25 minutes depending on your answers and interview type. Aim for balanced answers: enough detail to be credible, but concise enough to cover multiple questions.

09

What's in the PDF report I get after?

REPORTS add

Your report includes: Every question the AI asked (in order), Word-for-word transcription of your answers, Specific feedback on each answer (clarity, consistency, credibility), An overall score reflecting your preparation level, Key takeaways highlighting what to improve.

10

Can I download and save my report?

REPORTS add

Yes. Download your PDF, save it, and review it anytime. Keep reports from multiple attempts to track your improvement.

11

How many times can I retake an interview?

PRACTICE add

Unlimited. You can retake the same interview as many times as you want. This lets you directly measure improvement on the same questions.

12

Will the questions be different if I retake the same interview?

PRACTICE add

Some questions will be the same, but follow-up questions may differ based on your new answers. This is realistic - follow-ups in real interviews adapt to what you say.

13

Can I practice different universities with the same interview type?

SETUP add

Yes. You can practice Pre-CAS interviews for University A, then University B, then University C. Each will be customized to that university and course.

14

How many attempts should I do before my real interview?

PRACTICE add

Most students do 2-4 attempts before feeling genuinely ready. Your report will show if you're improving. If your score plateaus, you might need to research more or rethink your answers, not just practice more.

15

What score do I need to 'pass'?

REPORTS add

There's no passing score. Your score is a benchmark showing your current preparation level. The goal is to improve with each attempt until you feel genuinely confident.

16

Do you guarantee I'll pass my real interview?

POLICY add

No. Melete is a practice tool, not a guarantee. Your real interview depends on your university's or visa authority's decisions. Melete helps you prepare thoroughly so you walk in confident and ready.

17

Does using Melete guarantee admission, CAS issuance, or visa approval?

POLICY add

No. Universities, UKVI, and consulates make final decisions based on your complete application, academic record, and individual circumstances. Melete improves your preparation, not the outcome.

18

Is Melete an immigration advice service?

POLICY add

No. Melete is interview practice only. For visa rules, application requirements, legal questions, or immigration advice, consult official government sources (UKVI, US State Department, your embassy/consulate) or a qualified immigration professional.

19

Should I memorize my answers?

PRACTICE add

No. Memorization creates anxiety and backfires when you get unexpected questions. Instead, understand your story (why your university, why your course, how you're funding) so well that you can explain it naturally in your own words.

20

What if I give an answer I'm not happy with?

PRACTICE add

That's why you can retake. Your first attempt shows you where the gaps are. Use the feedback to improve, then practice again.
