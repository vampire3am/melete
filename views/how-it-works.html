5 Core Protocol

Start the Interview

Dynamic follow-up branches based on actual answers, not rigid scripted trees.

bolt 5 to 10 Adaptive Questions

The AI delivers your first inquiry. You listen, compose your structure, and answer aloud. The system captures your response in real-time. Crucially, Melete listens to the specific substance of your reply and generates tailored follow-up queries that compel you to defend, clarify, or extend your premises.

Why it works this way:

Reading passive question lists creates the illusion of mastery. Answering aloud under low-stakes pressure transforms passive recall into functional composure. Follow-up friction tests your true command of your documentation.

Phase 06 6

Submit Your Interview

When all inquiry sequences are satisfied, conclude and submit. The engine aggregates audio traces, syntactic clarity indices, and transcriptions for analysis.

Candidate Dossier

MSc Advanced Computing • Imperial College London

Pass Rating: 84 / 100
Question 03 [Adaptive Branch] Audio Length: 48s

"Why this specific module structure over comparable degrees in your home region?"

Verbatim Candidate Transcript

"The Distributed Systems laboratory at Imperial directly supports my work on consensus algorithms... however, uhm, local universities only offer standard database management and don't maintain active research labs in fault-tolerant architecture."

Alignment

High. Identified specific facilities and modules without falling into promotional generalities.

Fluency & Delivery

Moderate hesitation at second premise (1.8s pause). Eliminate filler 'uhm' token.

Direct Action

Mention the specific lab director or publication to solidify credibility on attempt 2.

Why This Works

Why Practicing Out Loud Works

Visa and university interviews are oral conversations. Memorizing fixed scripts breaks when interviewers ask unexpected questions. Practicing out loud helps you understand your own facts and answer naturally.

record_voice_over

Active Practice, Not Silent Reading

Reading sample answers in your head feels easy. But saying them out loud to another person is completely different. Melete gets you comfortable speaking your answers clearly.
