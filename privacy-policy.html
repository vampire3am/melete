Statutory Transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 16 September 2026

1. Local-First Processing

Melete operates with high data minimization standards. Spoken voice capture and real-time audio analysis are processed inside your browser session. We do not store persistent voice recordings on external cloud servers without explicit candidate consent.

2. Candidate Intake Data

Information entered during session parameterization (target university, course title, and funding source) is used solely to generate contextual interview inquiry branches and performance assessment dossiers.

3. Zero Data Sharing with Immigration Authorities

Melete is strictly an independent practice tool. Your interview transcripts, practice scores, and diagnostic reports are never shared with UKVI, the U.S. Department of State, foreign consulates, or educational institutions.

4. Rights & Data Deletion

Candidates maintain full sovereignty over their diagnostic reports. You can clear your session cache or download offline PDF copies at any time.
